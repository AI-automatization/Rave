# WeWatch — Veo (Gemini) video promptlari · UGC uslubi · kengaytirilgan

**Format:** vertikal 9:16 (720×1280), 3 klip × 10 soniya = 30 soniya
**Uslub:** UGC selfie-vlog — do'st gapirayotgandek, reklama emas
**Manba:** Jasurning ishlagan videosi (`video_is_very_cool_and_he_spe.mp4`) tahlilidan

> [!warning] Nima ishlamaganini yodda tut
> Birinchi urinishda kinematik qorong'i konsept yozilgan edi — «yigit yolg'iz
> o'tiradi, zerikkan». Veo buni so'zma-so'z bajardi: 10 soniya davomida
> harakat nol. **10 soniyalik formatda atmosfera qurilmaydi** — odam, gap va
> energiya kerak. Shuningdek `no on-screen text` cheklovi keraksiz edi:
> Veo `@wewatch.uz` matnini toza chizadi.

> [!tip] Nega blok tartibi aynan shunday
> Veo uzun promptda birinchi ~150 so'zga eng ko'p e'tibor beradi, oxirgi
> bloklar sustroq bajariladi. Shuning uchun **SUBJECT / ACTION / DIALOGUE**
> eng tepada, rang-tekstura-texnika esa pastda. Bloklarni joyini
> almashtirmang — tartibning o'zi ishlaydi.

## KLIP 1 — Hook (0:00–0:10)

```
[SCENE OVERVIEW]
A 10-second vertical 9:16 selfie-vlog clip in the style of authentic
user-generated content filmed on a modern smartphone. A young man talks
directly into his front-facing camera on a sunny city street, sharing an
exciting discovery with the viewer as if speaking to a close friend. The
entire clip is one continuous unbroken take with no cuts, no transitions and
no camera changes. The energy is warm, casual, spontaneous and genuinely
enthusiastic — this must never look like a produced television commercial.

[SUBJECT — exact appearance, keep byte-identical across all three clips]
A 24-year-old Uzbek man, Central Asian features, warm light-olive skin tone,
short dark brown hair neatly styled with a little natural volume at the front,
light well-groomed stubble along the jaw, thick dark expressive eyebrows, dark
brown eyes, straight nose, an open friendly face with a wide natural smile that
shows his upper teeth. Athletic-slim build, average height, relaxed confident
posture. He holds a black smartphone at arm's length in his right hand,
slightly above his own eye level, angled down toward his face. His left hand is
free and gestures naturally while he speaks.

[WARDROBE]
A clean cream-beige cotton hoodie with a slightly oversized relaxed fit, hood
resting flat behind his neck, white drawstrings hanging evenly down the chest,
soft fabric with visible cotton texture. No visible brand logos, no prints, no
patterns. Nothing else in frame — no bag, no jewellery, no watch, no hat, no
sunglasses.

[LOCATION]
A wide clean sunny city street in Tashkent in front of a modern high-rise office
building with a large reflective blue-glass facade. Behind him the background is
softly out of focus: glass panels catching the sunlight, a strip of green trees,
a light-grey paved sidewalk, distant blurred pedestrians far away and out of
focus. The background is bright, airy, open and clearly urban, but never busy or
distracting. Nobody walks close to him, nobody enters the frame.

[TIME & WEATHER]
Late morning on a clear day. Bright open sky, no visible clouds, no rain, no
wind. Warm sunlight, comfortable weather.

[ACTION — beat by beat, ten seconds total]
0.0–1.0s — He is already mid-motion, looking straight into the lens with a wide
excited smile, head tilted very slightly, as if the camera caught him already
talking. He must never start from a still neutral pose.
1.0–3.5s — He raises his eyebrows and opens his free left hand toward the camera
in an inviting gesture as he begins the first sentence.
3.5–6.5s — He nods twice while speaking, his smile widening, and briefly glances
off to his left as if picturing his friend, then returns his eyes to the lens.
6.5–9.0s — He leans a few centimetres closer to the camera on the question,
tilting his head with a playful raised eyebrow, clearly inviting an answer.
9.0–10.0s — He holds the smile and eye contact, giving one small confident nod,
still fully alive and animated as the clip ends. He must not freeze.

[DIALOGUE — spoken in Uzbek, accurately lip-synced]
He says, clearly and with natural enthusiasm, in Uzbek:
"Do'stim boshqa shaharda, lekin biz har kuni kechqurun birga kino ko'ramiz.
Qanaqasiga deysizmi?"

[PERFORMANCE NOTES]
Delivery is fast, warm and conversational, the natural rhythm of a friend
telling good news, never a rehearsed announcer read. Micro-expressions
throughout: a genuine smile reaching the eyes with slight crinkling at the outer
corners, small eyebrow lifts on the stressed words, natural blinking, tiny
believable head movements. His free hand keeps moving in small natural
gestures — it must never hang motionless. The final question is delivered with
rising playful intonation.

[CAMERA]
Selfie POV from the phone's front-facing camera held in his own hand. Wide-angle
front lens with the mild characteristic barrel distortion of a phone selfie
camera. Framing is a medium close-up from mid-chest up, subject centred
horizontally with his eyes positioned in the upper third of the vertical frame.
Camera is slightly above eye level looking gently down at him. Constant subtle
handheld micro-shake from his hand, plus tiny natural drift and re-framing —
never tripod-stable, never robotically smooth. No zoom, no pan, no dolly, no
cuts, no orbit, no slow motion, no speed ramp. Shallow natural depth of field
keeping his face sharp while the background stays softly blurred.

[LIGHTING]
Bright natural daylight only, no artificial or studio lighting. Open sunlight
acts as the key light on his face from slightly in front and above, soft and
even with gentle shadow under the jaw. Sunlight bouncing off the pale pavement
provides soft natural fill from below. The glass building behind reflects a soft
cool rim of light onto his shoulders and hair, separating him from the
background. Highlights on the glass facade are slightly blown out, exactly as a
phone camera behaves in bright sun.

[COLOR & TEXTURE]
Natural true-to-life color, warm and clean, no heavy grading. Skin tones healthy
and accurate. Blue-glass building and green trees give a cool background that
contrasts with his warm cream hoodie and warm skin. Mild lens flare from the sun
is welcome. Slight digital sharpening and light noise in the shadows, as real
phone footage has. Sharp, crisp, high-resolution modern smartphone image
quality — not filmic, not cinematic, not graded.

[ON-SCREEN GRAPHICS]
The text "@wewatch.uz" in a clean white sans-serif font, medium weight,
horizontally centred near the bottom of the vertical frame, with a soft subtle
drop shadow for readability against the bright background. The text is static,
perfectly spelled and correctly rendered. Nothing else appears on screen.

[AUDIO]
His clear natural male voice speaking Uzbek at conversational volume, recorded
close on the phone microphone with natural presence. Quiet ambient city
atmosphere underneath: distant traffic, faint birds, a soft breeze. No music, no
sound effects, no voice-over narration, no echo, no reverb.

[REALISM ANCHORS]
Everything must read as a real person filming a real phone video: natural
imperfect framing, believable skin with visible pores and texture, real fabric
folds in the hoodie, correct human hand anatomy with five fingers gripping the
phone, natural eye reflections, physically consistent shadows.

[NEGATIVE PROMPT]
no subtitles, no captions, no burned-in text other than @wewatch.uz, no
watermarks, no logos, no other people in frame, no camera cuts, no transitions,
no zoom, no slow motion, no music, no studio lighting, no green screen, no
indoor setting, no night scene, no tripod stability, no distorted hands, no
extra fingers, no deformed face, no text artifacts, no blurry face, no
motionless posing, no cinematic color grade, no letterbox bars, no horizontal
framing.
```
