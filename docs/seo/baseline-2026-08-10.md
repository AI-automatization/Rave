Three findings:

1. **Uzbek is already won.** Google AI gives WeWatch its own regional category and cites nothing
   but our own pages.
2. **The Russian answer mis-categorises us as a mobile app.** That matches what the old build
   publishes — `MobileApplication` schema, `operatingSystem: "iOS, Android"`, and a meta
   description ending in "Скачай на iOS и Android". `main` already replaces all three with
   `SoftwareApplication` / `operatingSystem: 'Web'` and removes every store mention, so this is a
   direct, testable prediction: **the category placement should change after deploy.**
3. **English and Perplexity cite third-party sources, not vendor pages** — Reddit threads and
   round-up articles. Indexing is not the blocker there (Bing has 26 pages); third-party
   mentions are.
