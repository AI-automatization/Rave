  Cinematic product advertisement video for a mobile app called "WeWatch" —
  a social platform for watching videos together with friends.

  SCENE SETUP:
  Dark, moody studio environment. Deep black background with subtle purple and
  violet volumetric light rays. Floating iPhone 17 Pro in titanium black finish,
  perfectly centered, slight 3D rotation (7-degree tilt), professional product
  photography lighting with rim light on the edges.
