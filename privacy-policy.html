Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

When you use WeWatch, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account data: email address, username, and profile photo you provide during registration.
  • Usage data: watch history, watch party activity, and interaction with other users (friends, reactions).
  • Device data: push notification tokens (Expo/FCM) used solely to deliver notifications to your device.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, and operating system for security and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and improve the WeWatch service.
  • To send push notifications about friend requests, watch party invites, and app activity.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, spam, and abuse.
  • To personalise your experience (recommended content, watch history).

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with:

  • Firebase (Google) — for push notification delivery.
  • Expo — for push notification token management.
  • Sentry — for crash reporting (no personally identifiable information is sent).
  • Law enforcement when required by applicable law.

4. Data Retention

Your account data is retained for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Settings screen.

5. Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS, JWT RS256 authentication, bcrypt password hashing, and rate limiting to protect your data.

6. Children's Privacy

WeWatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, use the account deletion feature in the app or contact us.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via in-app notification.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@wewatch.app.
